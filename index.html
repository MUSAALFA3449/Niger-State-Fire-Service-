About Us - Established 1st July 1977

OUR VISION: To be the most effective, efficient, reliable and transparent Fire Service in Nigeria.

OUR MISSION: Saving of lives and properties in terms of controlling, preventing, extinguishing of fire outbreak throughout the state.

CORE MANDATE: Fire Fighting, Rescue Operation, Fire Prevention, Enforcement, Inspection, Fire Safety Training, Fire Investigation, Humanitarian Services.
