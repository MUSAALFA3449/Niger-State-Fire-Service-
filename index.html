📜 Our History 1977 - 2026

Historical Development, Achievements and Transformation

Niger State Fire Service was Established 1st July 1977.

The Niger State Fire Service is a government emergency-response agency responsible for protecting lives, property and the environment against fire outbreaks and related emergencies across Niger State.

Its responsibilities include firefighting, rescue operations, fire prevention, public enlightenment, fire-safety inspections and collaboration with other emergency-management institutions.

1st July 1977 - Establishment of Niger State Fire Service
Niger State Fire Service was established on 1st July 1977 to provide organised fire protection, fire fighting and rescue services across Niger State as towns, markets and communities expanded.
5th March 2013 - Decoration of 36 Promoted Fire Officers
Decoration of 36 Promoted Fire Officers at Abubakar Tafyan Hall, Hassan Ndakogi Fire Academy, Minna. The Academy and Hall are named in honour of past leaders Hassan Ndakogi and Abubakar Tafyan.
12th October 2015 - 7th National Council on Fire Conference, Niger 2015
Niger State hosted the 7th National Council on Fire Conference, Niger 2015, on 12/10/2015, bringing together Fire Service leaders from across Nigeria.
2016 - 2018 - Infrastructure Expansion
In December 2016, the administration of Governor Abubakar Sani Bello announced plans to improve fire-service infrastructure. Reported plans included six new fire stations, 13 firefighting engines, water-storage facilities, industrial boreholes and backup water tankers in Minna, Bida, Suleja, Kontagora, Mokwa and other towns.
2019 - 2020 - Rehabilitation
Rehabilitation and equipping work at stations in Minna, Bida, Mokwa and Kontagora, as well as construction at Lapai. The then Director was Alhaji Mohammed Baba Kudu.
2019 - Federal Fire Service Zone G
The Federal Government announced a Federal Fire Service zonal office in Minna, designated Zone G, covering Niger, Kogi and Kwara States. The Federal and State Services are separate institutions, but collaborate during emergencies.
2021 - Reforms and Achievements
Reconstruction and equipping of ten fire stations across ten Local Government Areas, recruitment of additional personnel, improvements in welfare, and alignment of salary structure with the Federal Fire Service.
Courtesy Visit - CG Federal Fire Service & Controller Niger State
Courtesy Visit of the Controller General of Federal Fire Service, Alh. Liman Ibrahim, and Controller Niger State Fire Service, Alh. Bello Salihu, during the courtesy visit to the Niger State Gov't House, Alh. Sani Bello.
2022 - 2025 - Modernisation
Focus on modern firefighting methods, training and retraining, vehicle maintenance, emergency communication and incident records.
2026 - Recent Developments
Acquisition of six fire trucks, procurement of two quick-intervention vehicles for markets, recruitment and absorption of casual personnel with a reported workforce of 468 personnel, repairs to four trucks, drilling of a borehole at Headquarters, and improvement of the control room.

🔥 Fire Safety Tips - Protect Your Family

  1. Switch off electrical appliances when leaving home.
  2. Do not leave candle, lantern or stove unattended.
  3. Keep matches away from children.
  4. Do not store fuel inside house or shop.
  5. Teach children not to play with fire.
  6. Know your nearest Fire Station.
  7. When you see fire, call 08062420591, 07013132402 immediately.
  8. Do not block Fire Service vehicles. Give us way.
