📜 About Us / Our History

Our History: Niger State Fire Service is the fire and rescue authority for Niger State, Headquarters in Minna. Established by the Niger State Government to protect lives and property.

From a small fire station in Minna with few firemen and one fire truck, the Service has grown to stations across Minna, Bida, Kontagora, Suleja, New Bussa, Lapai, Mokwa, Kagara and others.

Mission: To prevent, control and extinguish fires, rescue victims, educate public on fire safety.

Vision: A Niger State where every home, market, school, office is safe from fire disaster.

Core Values: Bravery, Discipline, Service, Speed, Sacrifice.

Our Promise: Whenever you call us, we will come. Whenever there is fire, we will fight it. Whenever there is danger, we will rescue.

🔥 Fire Safety Tips - Protect Your Family

  1. Switch off electrical appliances when leaving home.
  2. Do not leave candle, lantern or stove unattended.
  3. Keep matches away from children.
  4. Do not store fuel inside house or shop.
  5. Teach children not to play with fire.
  6. Know your nearest Fire Station.
  7. When you see fire, call 08062420591 immediately.
  8. Do not block Fire Service vehicles. Give us way.
