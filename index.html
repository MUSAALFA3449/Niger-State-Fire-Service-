📜 Our History 1977 - 2026

📖 Historical Development, Achievements and Transformation

📅 Niger State Fire Service was Established 1st July 1977.

Government emergency-response agency protecting lives, property and environment across Niger State.

📅 1st July 1977 - Establishment
Established to provide fire protection, fire fighting and rescue.
🏅 5th March 2013 - Decoration of 36 Officers
At Abubakar Tafyan Hall, Hassan Ndakogi Fire Academy, Minna.
🎤 12th Oct 2015 - 7th National Council on Fire
Niger State hosted it.
🏗️ 2016-2018 - Expansion
Six new stations, 13 engines, water storage in Minna, Bida, Suleja, Kontagora, Mokwa.
🔧 2019-2020 - Rehabilitation
Minna, Bida, Mokwa, Kontagora, Lapai.
🤝 2019 - Federal Fire Service Zone G
Zone G in Minna covering Niger, Kogi, Kwara.
📈 2021 - Reforms
Reconstruction of ten stations, recruitment, welfare.
💻 2022-2025 - Modernisation
Modern methods, training, communication.
🚒 2026 - Recent
Six fire trucks, two quick-intervention vehicles, 468 personnel, borehole at HQ, improved control room.

🔥 Fire Safety Tips - Protect Your Family

  1. 🔌 Switch off appliances when leaving home.
  2. 🕯️ Do not leave candle, lantern or stove unattended.
  3. 👶 Keep matches away from children.
  4. ⛽ Do not store fuel inside house or shop.
  5. 🚫 Teach children not to play with fire.
  6. 📍 Know your nearest Fire Station.
  7. 📞 When you see fire, call 08062420591 / 07013132402 immediately.
  8. 🚒 Do not block Fire Service vehicles. Give us way.
