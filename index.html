📜 Our History

Niger State Fire Service was established to protect lives and property in Niger State. From its headquarters on Father O'Connell Road, Minna, our men and women have served with courage, responding to fire outbreaks, rescues and emergencies across the state.

We continue to grow, train and serve under the leadership of Controller Usman F. Usman MFPAN.

Mission: To prevent, control and extinguish fires, rescue victims, and educate the public on fire safety.

Vision: A Niger State where every home, market, school and community is safe from fire disaster.

🔥 Safety Tips

  • Never leave cooking gas or stove unattended.
  • Switch off electrical appliances when not in use.
  • Keep matches away from children.
  • Have a fire extinguisher at home.
  • If there is fire, leave immediately and call 08062420591.
